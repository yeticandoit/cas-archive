THE SOUND

Quiet music.
Heavy atmosphere.

Cigarettes After Sex emerged from Greg Gonzalez's recordings and developed into a band whose sound is immediately recognizable for its dreamy guitar textures, restrained rhythm section and intimate delivery.

The project has moved from early releases such as I. and Affection through the self-titled debut, Cry and X's, while continuing to collect standalone releases and covers.
